About the beta

Sophis is an experimental nutrition tracking app provided by Mohammed Hamid. Beta versions may contain errors, change or become unavailable. Keep backups of information you need.

Review nutrition estimates

Nutrition values and AI estimates can be inaccurate. Review them before saving. Sophis is not a medical device or a substitute for professional medical advice.

External services & sharing
