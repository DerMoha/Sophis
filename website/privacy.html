Privacy policy

Updated 3 November 2026

The essentials

Sophis is a nutrition tracking app provided by Mohammed Hamid. Core tracking requires no account. Optional partner sharing uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. Sophis does not use advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics.

For support and privacy requests, contact sophis@brokenmoha.de.

Information on your device

Nutrition goals and logs, water and weight entries, workouts, meal plans, recipes, supplements, preferences, cached barcode results and progress photos are stored locally using SQLite, app files and preferences. User-provided AI keys and optional Open Food Facts credentials use the operating system’s secure credential storage.

Nutrition-label scanning begins with local text recognition and parsing. The original image is not uploaded during this local pass. Captured label images may also be saved to the system Photos library; those copies can be managed there.

Apple Health data is read or written only with the relevant permission. Notifications and biometric protection for progress photos are handled locally by the operating system.

Optional partner sharing

Creating an account sends your email and authentication credentials to Firebase Authentication. Your profile contains a display name and account identifier. Connecting a partner shares diary days, including food entries, nutrition goals and burned calories, through Cloud Firestore. The app synchronizes the most recent seven diary days. Your connected partner can access shared information.

Disconnecting removes the shared day documents and connection. Deleting the partner account within the app also removes your profile, invitations and Firebase account. Removing local app data alone does not delete the remote account or copies another person has retained. Google controls Firebase processing under its terms and privacy policy.

Optional external features

  • Food search sends search terms to Open Food Facts. Barcode lookup sends barcodes to Open Food Facts and, if needed, Brocade. Results may be cached locally.
  • AI food, recipe and image features send selected images or text directly to the provider you choose: Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Anthropic or a custom compatible endpoint, using your own key.
  • If cloud help for labels is enabled, unclear label images and/or OCR text may be sent to the selected provider. Review AI estimates before saving.
  • Submitting a product to Open Food Facts sends the product data and any entered credentials to that service.

AI and food lookup requests go directly to the selected services. Partner sharing separately uses Firebase. External providers control their own processing and retention. Sophis does not use health, fitness or photo data for advertising, marketing or tracking.

Photos, sharing & backups

Camera and photo-library permissions support barcode, label, meal and progress-photo features. Progress photos stay local unless you choose to export or share them. The operating system’s share sheet sends selected content to the app or recipient you choose.

You can export a backup containing nutrition records and progress-photo files. You control exported files after they leave Sophis.

Deletion, retention & security

Delete All Local Data removes Sophis records, app-owned progress photos, settings, reminders and saved credentials from the app. It does not remove exported or shared copies, label images in Photos, or the remote partner account. You can remove saved API keys and Open Food Facts credentials in Settings.

Operating-system backups follow the platform’s retention rules. Requests to external providers may be retained according to their policies. Shared partner data remains in Firebase until it is removed through the partner/account deletion flows. Credentials use secure operating-system storage and remote connections are encrypted.

Your choices & contact

The beta is intended for adults and provides general nutrition tracking, not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional for medical or dietary decisions.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete information and complain to your data protection authority. Email the maintainer for privacy requests; do not post sensitive details in public issues. Policy changes are published here with an updated date.
